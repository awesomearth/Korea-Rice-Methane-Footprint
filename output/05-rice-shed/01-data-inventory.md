## How each file was obtained

- **KOSIS tables** (`code/kosis_dl.py`). No API key and no login. The script replays what the KOSIS table viewer sends when you click 다운로드 → CSV:
  1. read the table's default item and region lists from `statHtmlContent.do`;
  2. render the grid (`html.do`), which issues the `logSeq` token;
  3. `downGrid.do` returns a temporary file name;
  4. `downNormal.do` serves the CSV.

  Each CSV has a `.meta.json` sidecar with the table name, period coverage, KOSIS renewal date and item list. Region codes are KOSIS's own 5-digit codes (e.g. `31010 수원시`), not MOIS 행정표준코드.
- **Population** (`code/datagokr_dl.py`). The current data.go.kr version is `_20260930`. The `_20251231` version was taken from the dataset's "주기성 과거 데이터" list (56 versions; row `data-public-pk = uddi:4ade01ad-…`). It downloads without login through the portal's `selectFileDataDownload.do` → `fileDownload.do` flow. The file is CP949-encoded.
- **IPCC 2006 Vol. 4 Ch. 5**: a direct PDF download.
- **GIR NID 2025**: the attachment on the GIR board post (11,047,947 bytes, as listed on the post). The PDF opened, and section 5.4 (벼재배, PDF pp. 283–293) is readable. Phase 3 can therefore use the national rice factors as well as the IPCC defaults.

## Resolution — approved substitute (study owner decision, 2026-10-17)

The study owner approved **geoBoundaries gbOpen KOR ADM2** as the boundary input in place of S-BND (row 5′ above).

**The substitute file**
- **What it is:** 228 polygons. The metadata says `admUnitCount` 229; 228 are in the file. Names are romanized only (`shapeName`, e.g. "Jongno-gu", "Jung-gu [Central District]"), with no administrative codes.
- **Vintage:** boundaryYear 2020.
- **Licence:** CC BY 3.0. The source is citypopulation.de, per the file metadata; the geoBoundaries code is CC BY 4.0.
- **Citation:** Runfola D. et al. (2020), geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries, *PLoS ONE* 15(4): e0231866, plus citypopulation.de as the boundary source (`data/raw/boundary/gb/CITATION-AND-USE-geoBoundaries.txt`).
- **No login** was needed. Files come from the geoBoundaries API's `staticDownloadLink` (GitHub release commit `9469f09`).

- **Patch for a missing unit — 영광군 (Yeonggwang-gun, 전라남도).** geoBoundaries has 228 polygons against 229 units in its own `admUnitCount`; 영광군 is the one missing. Without it, 영광군's rice production and population could not be placed, and the mass balance would break. Its polygon was taken from **OpenStreetMap relation 7624187** (Nominatim, ODbL 1.0, no login; `data/raw/boundary/osm/yeonggwang_nominatim.json`).
  - The OSM boundary extends into the sea (2,105 km²), so it was clipped to land (ADM1 polygons) and to the gap between existing polygons, giving 397 km².
  - Its neighbours are Gochang-gun, Jangseong-gun and Hampyeong-gun, as expected.
  - Details: `04-flows.md` §1. This patch extends the approved substitution and is flagged here for the study owner.

**How it differs from S-BND**
1. **Unit level.** The polygons are at local-government level: non-autonomous gu (일반구) such as 수원시 장안구 are not separate polygons. Consuming units that are 일반구 take their parent city's polygon and centroid (Phase 4 crosswalk).
2. **No codes.** The join is by name. Two auxiliary inputs make it unambiguous, because romanized names repeat (6 × Jung-gu, 2 × Goseong-gun, …):
   - **geoBoundaries KOR ADM1** (row 5″, Public Domain, Natural Earth 2021) gives each ADM2 polygon's province, via its representative point.
   - **SGIS 시군구경계영문** (data.go.kr 15135856, keyless) gives the official Korean↔English si/gun/gu names.
3. **Vintage.** The boundaries are 2020, against 2025 attribute data. 군위군 moved from 경상북도 to 대구광역시 on 2023-07-01; its polygon is unchanged and is assigned to 대구 by the crosswalk.

`00-scope.md` (e) records the substitution.

## Data caveats found on download

- **Metropolitan cities are single producing units.** DT_1ET0034 reports 서울, 부산, 대구, 인천, 광주, 대전, 울산 and 세종 only as a 소계 row, with no gu split. Provinces are split by si/gun.
- **Production is already milled rice.** DT_1ET0034's title is "시군별 논벼 생산량(정곡, 92.9%)", i.e. 정곡 at a 92.9% milling rate. See `00-scope.md` (c).
- **Consumption is household consumption only.** DT_1ED0001 measures household consumption (전가구/농가/비농가). It excludes rice used in processing (가공용), food service and other non-household uses. This matters for the supply–demand balance (`00-scope.md` (b), Phase 2).
- **Population date.** Population is the 2025-12-31 resident registration count. The 2025 rice and consumption figures are annual totals.
