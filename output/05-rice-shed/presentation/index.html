    Whose rice, whose methane?

    Flooded paddies emit methane. This study traces it from 156 rice-producing areas to the 252 si/gun/gu that eat the rice.

    ↓→ next · ↑← back · or open the Index
    02 · The question 연구 질문

    A method built for US meat, applied to Korean rice

    Goldstein et al. (2025) found US city meat footprints track where the meat comes from (r = 0.99), and barely track how much people eat (r = −0.04).

    Here: where is the methane in each Korean unit's rice emitted, and does local sourcing change its footprint?

    Paper figures: 01-method-summary.md §1, §4. Korean figures: bundle.json (from data/processed/).
